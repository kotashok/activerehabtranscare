Total Knee Replacement (TKR)

Evidence-Based Post-Operative Rehabilitation Roadmap

Phase 1: Acute Healing & Mobility

Days 1–14
Clinical Focus: Edema Control & Passive Extension
  • Achieve complete knee extension (0°) and 90° of knee flexion.
  • Swelling control using ice therapy and continuous elevation.
  • Ankle pumps & quad sets to restore initial muscle firing.
  • Gentle passive heel slides and patella mobility drills.

Phase 2: Gait & Balance Restoration

Weeks 2–6
Clinical Focus: Limp Reversal & Stance Symmetry
  • Increase knee flexion past 110° and normalize walking gait.
  • Parallel rail support drills to eliminate protective limping.
  • Stance re-education using high-density foam balance pods.
  • Progressive weight-bearing and light resistance band exercises.

Phase 3: Strength & Proprioception

Weeks 6–12
Clinical Focus: Muscle Volume & Joint Control
  • Achieve full range of motion (flexion past 120°+).
  • Closed-chain exercises (squats, step-ups) to rebuild quadriceps volume.
  • Advanced coordination drills to restore proprioception.
  • Safe, pain-free stair climbing and returning to daily activities.

Key Recovery Rules

R.I.C.E. Protocol Integration

Apply cold packs for 20 minutes every 2 hours during the first 14 days to minimize surgical inflammation and deep tissue heating.

Extension is Priority #1

Keeping the knee straight (0° extension) is critical. Never place a pillow directly under the knee joint; place it under the ankle instead.

Calibrated Weight-Bearing

Gradually load weight as tolerated under therapist guidance to build joint confidence and stimulate bone remodeling.

Red Flag Warnings

Seek immediate clinical assessment if you experience sudden calf pain/redness (deep vein thrombosis sign), localized heat, or leaking surgical wounds.
