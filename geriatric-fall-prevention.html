Geriatric & Fall Prevention

Reclaiming confidence in every step. We provide a warm, professional, and monitored environment dedicated to reversing muscular sarcopenia, correcting senile gait instability, and ensuring complete safety and independence for our elders.

Clinical Scope

Target Conditions Treated

Senile Gait & Balance Instability

Center-of-gravity recalibration, stride length optimization, base-of-support correction, and sensory-motor walking reconditioning.

Osteoporosis & Post-Fall Care

Low-impact safe bone loading, dynamic posture alignment monitoring, muscular splinting, and progressive weight-bearing balance exercises.

Vestibular Vertigo & Dizziness

Inner-ear repositioning procedures (Epley maneuver), Gaze Stabilization drills (VOR), and dynamic ocular-balance tracking.

Sarcopenia Muscle Reconditioning

Customized low-impact strength loading targeting calf muscles, quadriceps-hamstring chains, and core spinal stabilizers to reverse atrophy.

Cognitive-Motor Safety Training

Dual-task cognitive interference training (calculating or conversing while walking) to prevent senior attention-split fall accidents.

The TransCare Blueprint

Interactive Care Protocol

Days 1 - 5: Safety Setup & Vestibular Baseline

Conduct professional Environmental Fall Risk (EFPT) assessment, establish baseline vestibular/balance scores, initiate gentle posture flexibility, and introduce double-rail gait guidance.

Days 6 - 15: Strength & Stance Correction

Begin low-impact progressive muscle reconditioning to reverse sarcopenia, perform center-of-gravity posture drills, and introduce high-density foam balance platform exercises.

Weeks 3 - 4: Walk Confidence & Safety Pacing

Progress to advanced dual-task cognitive balance exercises, dynamic floor surface walking simulations, home safety transfer drills, and complete independent walking safety.

Environmental Framework & Pacing (EFPT)

Simulating real-world spatial challenges (thick carpets, stairs, cords, and multi-level thresholds) to train seniors to lift their feet properly and avoid common trip hazards.

Vestibular Ocular Reflex (VOR) & Balance

Specialized ocular tracking and quick head adjustment drills designed to eliminate inner-ear dizzy spells, stabilize gaze while walking, and reduce motion instability.

Sarcopenia Reversal & Core Stabilization

Targeted, low-impact clinical strengthening focused on ankle dorsiflexors, hip abductors, and core muscles to build a robust physiological foundation for standing.

High-Density Proprioceptive Foam Pods

Challenging soft balance pads that stimulate micro-sensory nerves in the soles of the feet, rapidly returning accurate joint position sense and stance correction.

Postural Alignment Mirrors & Stance Corridor

Double-sided parallel corridor rails facing full-length alignment mirrors, allowing seniors to visually adjust their walking posture, straight spine, and stride symmetries.

Dynamic Tilt Wobble Boards

Controlled instability platforms used under therapist supervision to retrain immediate reactive ankle adjustments, preparing seniors for unexpected slips or trips.
