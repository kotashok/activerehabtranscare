Focus: Orthopedic Recovery

Post-Op Ortho Recovery

Where clinical surgery ends, active mobility begins. We provide professional, 24/7 monitored step-down rehabilitation for joint replacements and spine surgeries, safeguarding your surgical outcome and returning you home with confidence.

Clinical Scope

Target Conditions Treated

Total Knee Replacement (TKR)

Automated Continuous Passive Motion (CPM) flex angles build, safe weight-bearing stance, and anti-inflammatory compression therapy.

Total Hip Replacement (THR)

Strict hip-precaution alignment monitoring, guided transfers, muscular reconditioning, and stable gait re-education.

Spine Surgery Post-Op

ACL/MCL Ligament Reconstruction

Bilateral limb stabilization, progressive muscle loading, proprioception drills, and dynamic knee flex angle reconditioning.

The TransCare Blueprint

Interactive Care Protocol

Days 1 - 3: Immediate Post-Op

Focus on safe bed-to-chair functional transfers, continuous pain monitoring, swelling management via automated water compression and cryotherapy, and gentle passive range of motion.

Days 4 - 10: Early Weight-Bearing

Guided weight shifting using parallel bars and walkers, active-assisted range of motion target drills, and progressive quadriceps/hamstrings isometrics to combat postoperative atrophy.

Weeks 2 - 4: Mobility Mastery

Complete independence in functional transfers, light resistance band exercises, stance correction, and navigating household stairs safely under expert supervision before going home.

Continuous Passive Motion (CPM)

Utilizing automated mechanical knee flexion devices to systematically restore flex angles and prevent scar tissue/joint adhesions without active muscular strain.

Gait Retraining & Alignment

Proprietary stance and posture corrections to quickly reverse postoperative limps, balance the gait speed, and safeguard contralateral hip/knee joints.

Incision Line Protection & Wound Care

Trained nurses perform sterile medical dressings and provide 24/7 oversight to monitor suture integrity, warmth, and eliminate infection risks completely before sutures/staples are removed.

Advanced Cryotherapy Systems

Controlled cold compression machines that cycle iced water through custom joint sleeves, drastically reducing post-surgical swelling and localized pain.

Parallel Rails & Stance Corridors

Double-sided rehabilitation support structures dedicated to post-operative posture correction, guided safe steps, and slip-free balance training.

High-Density Standing Balance Pads

Specialized dense foam balance pods that challenge deep stability muscles and ankle joints, speeding up proprioceptive recovery.
