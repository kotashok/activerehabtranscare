Clinical Continuity & Security

ActiveRehab TransCare serves as an extension of your acute care services. We specialize in receiving patients immediately post-discharge, bridging the critical gap between hospital exit and independent home living.

1

Immediate Pre-Admission Audit

Our clinical team reviews discharge summaries, current vitals, and surgical notes to confirm eligibility and prepare dedicated suite telemetry.

2

Clinical Handover Checklist

Structured intake checking pain maps, wound integrity, telemetry requirements, medication regimes, and immediate mobility restrictions.

3

Dr. Ashok P. Kota's Protocol Integration

Daily customized physiotherapy (up to 3 hours), step-down mobility retraining, and active recovery suites under 24/7 clinical supervision.

4

Referral Loop Updates

We keep referring physicians updated with weekly progress telemetry reports, tracking joint range of motion (ROM) or gait metrics.
