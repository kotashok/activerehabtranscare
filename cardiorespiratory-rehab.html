Cardiorespiratory Rehab

Monitored pacing, robust recovery. We provide highly specialized 24/7 telemetry-monitored cardiac and respiratory rehabilitation to rebuild your physical capacity, strengthen lung function, and safely guide you back to active living.

Clinical Scope

Target Conditions Treated

Post-CABG (Bypass) Reconditioning

Strict telemetry-monitored progressive mobility, sternal healing protection drills, safe posture management, and lung recruitment breathing exercises.

Post-Angioplasty & Heart Attack Recovery

Structured Phase II cardiovascular endurance building, metabolic equivalent (MET) pacing, and supervised daily lifestyle changes coaching.

COPD & Chronic Asthma Care

Guided diaphragmatic breathing, pursed-lip expiration training, clinical airway clearance, and accessory neck/chest muscle tension relief.

Post-Pneumonia / Post-COVID Recovery

Incentive spirometry pacing, lower lung lobe recruitment, dynamic oxygenation monitoring, and progressive physical fatigue management.

Congestive Heart Failure (CHF) Care

Controlled step-down fluid/weight monitoring support, professional energy-conservation pacing, and gentle, non-fatiguing strength builds.

The TransCare Blueprint

Interactive Care Protocol

Days 1 - 5: Acute Monitored Reconditioning

Initiate regulated deep breathing re-education, monitored incentive spirometry, gentle non-weight-bearing joint mobility, and highly supervised transitions with telemetry support.

Days 6 - 15: Stamina & Aerobic Progression

Monitored telemetry corridor walking, step-up simulation training, functional endurance tasks (target 1-3 MET load), and professional thoracic incision stabilization chest exercises.

Weeks 3 - 4: Self-Management & Independence

Mastering home energy-conservation pacing, independent symptom monitoring (RPE scale, pulse oximetry thresholds), safe daily transfers, and complete functional independence.

Telemetry-Monitored Aerobic Progression

Strictly logging heart-rate reserves, continuous SpO2 readings, and blood pressure dynamics before, during, and after therapeutic physical loading to optimize safe pacing.

Sternal Protection & Incision Pacing

Educating patients on safe coughing using sternal splinting pillows, correct log-rolling bed transfers, and eliminating asymmetrical bilateral arm shear stress.

Diaphragmatic & Airway Clearance

Applying specialized chest vibration, posture drainage support, and targeted coughing cycles to clear secretions, expand lower lung lobes, and reduce daily shortness of breath.

Precision Clinical Pulse Oximeters

Professional patient-monitoring units that display rapid real-time arterial oxygen levels and heart rates during active training to eliminate clinical desaturation risk.

Incentive Spirometry Pacing Devices

Visual flow indicators that challenge and measure inhalation volume, helping open deep air sacs and prevent post-surgical chest congestion.

Custom Sternal Splinting Pillows

Ergonomically designed medical-grade chest support pillows that patients hold firmly during coughing or sneezing to secure their healing breastbone post-bypass.
