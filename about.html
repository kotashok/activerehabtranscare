About ActiveRehab TransCare

Hyderabad's premier step-down rehabilitation facility — where clinical excellence meets compassionate, round-the-clock care.

Our Story

Bridging the Gap Between Hospital and Home

ActiveRehab TransCare was founded with a single, clear purpose: to fill the critical gap that exists when a patient is discharged from hospital but is not yet ready to return home safely.

In India, thousands of post-surgical and post-stroke patients are discharged from acute hospitals every day into settings that cannot provide the level of medical monitoring, intensive physiotherapy, or 24/7 clinical supervision they still require. This gap leads to complications, readmissions, and prolonged disability.

Located adjacent to Sri Meru Superspeciality Hospital in Kompally, Hyderabad, ActiveRehab TransCare offers a medically supervised step-down environment where patients continue their recovery under the direct care of specialist physiotherapists and nursing staff — with the intensity of a clinical setting and the dignity of a premium private facility.

A Multi-Disciplinary Expert with International Training

Dr. Ashok P. Kota is one of the few physiotherapists in Hyderabad to hold internationally recognised postgraduate qualifications in both Osteopathy and Chiropractic alongside his core BPT training — bringing a uniquely integrated, multi-disciplinary approach to every patient's rehabilitation.

With over 17 years of hands-on clinical experience, Dr. Kota has managed complex neurological and orthopedic cases including post-stroke rehabilitation, total knee and hip replacement recovery, spinal cord injury rehab, Parkinson's disease management, and post-CABG cardiac reconditioning. His international training in Canada and Sweden has given him exposure to evidence-based rehabilitation models that go beyond conventional Indian physiotherapy protocols.

Our Advantage

Why ActiveRehab TransCare?

Internationally Trained Clinicians

Our lead physiotherapist holds postgraduate qualifications from Canada and Sweden — bringing evidence-based, internationally tested rehabilitation protocols to Hyderabad.

24/7 Medical Supervision

Unlike home physiotherapy or standard nursing homes, we provide round-the-clock nursing, telemetry monitoring, and on-call medical doctor support.

Adjacent to Sri Meru Hospital

Patients discharging from Sri Meru Superspeciality Hospital can transfer directly to our facility — minimising the risk and stress of long-distance transport post-surgery.

Deep Clinical Protocol Depth

We treat rare and complex conditions including GBS, post-COVID reconditioning, CHF management, and vestibular disorders — conditions most rehab facilities cannot handle.

Premium Private Environment

Single and double suites designed for comfort and dignity during recovery — not a ward environment, but a private, well-equipped space that patients and families can trust.

Personalised Recovery Pathways

Every patient receives a customised step-down care plan built by Dr. Kota based on their surgical history, mobility status, and recovery goals — not a one-size-fits-all protocol.
