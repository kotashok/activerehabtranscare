Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Step-Down Care Facility?

A step-down facility bridges the gap between an acute hospital stay and returning home. It provides medical monitoring, intense rehabilitation, and nursing care for patients who no longer need an ICU but aren't ready to go home safely.

Do you offer 24/7 nursing and medical support?

Yes. Our facility is equipped with 24/7 clinical monitoring, dedicated nursing staff, and on-call medical doctors to handle any emergencies or routine care needs.

What is the typical duration of stay?

The duration depends on the patient's condition and recovery goals. It typically ranges from 1 to 4 weeks for orthopedic surgeries, and longer for intensive neurological rehabilitation.
