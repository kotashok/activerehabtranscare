II. Scope of Clinical Care & Protocols

  • Skilled Nursing Care: 24/7 monitoring of vitals, sterile wound dressings, subcutaneous injections, and professional medication administration.
  • Active Physiotherapy: Joint mobilization, posture re-education, resistance training, and specialized clinical balance training.
  • Gait & Functional Mobility: Safe transfers practice, progressive corridor walking drills, and stair-climbing assessments.
  • Cardiorespiratory Support: Monitored oxygen therapy, incentive spirometry exercises, and telemetry pacing target metrics.

III. Anticipated Risks & Clinical Complications

Exercise Slips & Slips

Accidental slips or loss of balance during weight-bearing shifts or functional balance training despite therapist support.

Muscle Strains & Pain

Temporary post-exercise soreness, muscle spasms, localized joint swelling, or mild ligamentous strain during intensive stretching.

DVT & Pressure Ulcers

Deep vein thrombosis (blood clots in legs) or localized skin breakdown/pressure sores arising from prolonged recumbent bed rest.

Cardiac & Vital Strain

Temporary spike in blood pressure, heart rate pacing elevations, mild shortness of breath, or transient oxygen desaturations.

IV. Special Step-Down Facility Disclosures

Boundaries of Transitional Inpatient Care

The Guardian explicitly acknowledges that ActiveRehab TransCare is a specialized Step-Down Transitional Inpatient Care & Rehabilitation Facility and **NOT** a tertiary-care multi-specialty acute hospital. The facility is equipped with oxygen delivery lines, emergency airway carts, crash carts, portable ventilators, and resuscitation tools under on-site intensivists, but **DOES NOT possess an emergency surgical operating theater, emergency bypass surgery access, or invasive arterial hemodynamic lines**.

V. Emergency Tertiary Transfer Protocol

  • In the event of a severe, acute clinical deterioration or airway crisis that cannot be resolved on-site, the attending clinical team will immediately initiate resuscitation (CPR), high-flow oxygenation, and mechanical stabilization.
  • The facility will simultaneously initiate immediate emergency transit via an Advanced Life Support (ALS) Cardiac Ambulance equipped with transport ventilators and clinical escorts to a tertiary critical care facility.
